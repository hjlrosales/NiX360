# NiX 360 Media

A private, self-hosted photo and video library with 360° viewing, built for a Synology DS920+ and modern browsers.

**Status:** locally verified deployment candidate. The application, container image and Compose topology build and run locally; nothing has been deployed to the NAS, and no physical-device verification has happened yet. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for phases, [changelog.md](changelog.md) for exactly what was verified, and the [Synology deployment runbook](docs/synology-deployment.md) for the remaining operator gates.

## What works today

- Local accounts with Argon2id hashes, server-side sessions in `HttpOnly`/`SameSite=Strict` cookies, idle + absolute expiry, revocation on logout/disable/password change, login throttling and origin-based CSRF checks.
- Durable SQLite job queue with atomic claims, expiring leases, heartbeats, exponential backoff, permanent-vs-transient failures, crash recovery and manual retry/cancel.
- Read-only scanning of administratively configured media roots, with stability windows, Unicode paths, symlink refusal, source versioning and tombstones only after a full successful scan.
- Metadata probing (sharp for images, ffprobe for video, optional ExifTool for capture date/GPS), projection classification grounded in metadata rather than aspect ratio, direct-play detection.
- Thumbnail/poster/preview generation outside the request path, atomically published under versioned derivative directories and cleaned up when a source version changes.
- DJI OSV/LRF companion pairing: same-folder, same-stem files with agreeing durations become one recording, with a labeled LRF preview while the raw original's conversion state stays separate.
- Offline H.264 renditions for videos the browser cannot play directly (libx264 High@4.0, 8-bit 4:2:0, AAC, `+faststart`), verified with ffprobe before publication and served only when ready. Two versioned profiles exist — standard 1080p and a data-saver 720p — encoded only where they apply and offered through a **Quality** selector that keeps the playhead position.
- Tiled 360° photo viewing: versioned `pano-sq-v1` cube pyramids (2048 px faces, 512 px WebP tiles, 126 tiles) generated by the worker with ffmpeg `v360` + sharp, served through authorized per-tile routes and displayed by a lazily loaded Pannellum adapter with a flat-preview fallback.
- Administrator-saved 360° start views stored in `media_overrides` (audited, source untouched), plus an explicit projection override that re-queues tiles or withdraws them. The viewer distinguishes *tiles generating*, *tile generation failed* and *not tile-eligible* instead of one generic fallback.
- Recoverable panorama failures: a browser without WebGL, an unreachable tile set (a 20 s load watchdog, since Pannellum never reports it), a render error or a lost graphics context is explained inside the viewer with *Try interactive view again* and an explicit *Show flat preview*, and a restored context rebuilds the scene. Closing or replacing a viewer destroys it and its listeners.
- 360° viewer input: drag, two-finger pinch, wheel or key zoom, arrow-key pan, `[`/`]` item changes, orientation reset, optional motion control, and an immersive/fullscreen mode that keeps the toolbar and previous/next controls reachable.
- Explicit HDR handling: PQ, HLG and BT.2020 sources are identified from their declared transfer and primaries (not bit depth alone), are never offered as direct-play, and are encoded through a documented tone-map into BT.709. The viewer labels the conversion instead of presenting a washed-out or clipped SDR file as the original.
- Explicit spherical handling: every playback option reports the projection it actually serves, and an equirectangular rendition must keep its 2:1 frame or generation fails instead of publishing a flattened sphere. Spherical video plays inside an in-page WebGL sphere with drag, pinch, wheel and keyboard navigation plus play, seek, mute and immersive controls; a browser without WebGL falls back to the flat video with native controls.
- DJI export path: a full-sphere export imported into a media root can be linked to its raw `.OSV` in **Admin → Spherical exports**. The link records both source versions, the acting administrator and a policy version in `media_relations`; the recording then plays through the export while the raw original stays conversion-required, and a change to either file suspends the link until it is confirmed again.
- Authorized delivery of every representation (original, thumbnail, preview, poster, panorama tile) with HTTP Range, `HEAD`, ETag revalidation and `private, no-store` caching.
- Proxy-ready HTTPS posture: `Secure` `HttpOnly` `SameSite=Strict` session cookies, configurable trusted-proxy handling, origin-checked state changes, HSTS, a documented Nginx/DSM reverse-proxy topology and an automated local TLS-terminator test.
- Per-user video resume: the saved position is returned with media detail, resumed only when it is safely inside the clip, saved every 5 s and on pause/close, and reset when the video finishes.
- Installable PWA: manifest, maskable icons, an app-shell service worker, update prompt, offline messaging and platform-specific install guidance. Offline thumbnails are **opt-in**, account-scoped, bounded to 500 items / 100 MB / 30 days, and purged on sign-out or account switch. Other API responses, videos and originals are never cached by the worker.
- NiX light-theme shell: Home, Photos, Videos, 360° Media, Albums, Favorites, Recently Viewed, Settings, Admin, with loading/empty/error/unsupported states.
- Disk-reserve protection: thumbnail, encode and tile jobs refuse to start below `NIX_MIN_FREE_MB`, retry later and never publish partial output. A worker restart reconciles missing derivative files and re-queues a bounded regeneration set.
- Raw DJI `.OSV` files are catalogued and shown as *conversion required* until a validated stitching route exists.

Deliberately not implemented yet: HLS/adaptive streaming packaging (Phase 5), remote-access configuration and offline catalog browsing (Phase 6), uploads, sharing links, facial recognition, maps. Tiled 360° viewing and its input modes (drag, pinch, keyboard, immersive, previous/next) have been exercised in desktop and headless Chrome, but not on a phone or tablet. Spherical video navigation passed headless-Chrome checks against a generated equirectangular clip; a real DJI export, device playback and audio sync are still unverified.

## DJI LRF companion previews

Standalone `.LRF` probing, poster generation, and authorized delivery have local test evidence. Automatic pairing now associates a validated LRF with its OSV, presents one recording, and prefers the LRF for preview playback without encoding. The raw OSV remains preserved and conversion-required; spherical LRF playback, browser playback and physical-device acceptance are **not verified**. See the [LRF preview specification](docs/lrf-preview.md) for the contract and remaining checks.

## Requirements

- Node.js 24 or newer (the server runs TypeScript directly through Node's type stripping; no build step for the backend).
- `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` on `PATH` for video posters and probing.
- Optional: `exiftool` for image capture dates and GPS. Without it, image dates fall back to file modification time and are marked uncertain.
- Docker Compose for deployment; the image installs ffmpeg and exiftool.

## Local development

```bash
npm install
cp .env.example .env          # adjust paths; do not commit it
npm run migrate               # create data/library.sqlite
NIX_ADMIN_LOGIN=owner NIX_ADMIN_PASSWORD='a long passphrase' npm run bootstrap-admin
npm run dev:web               # http://127.0.0.1:5173 (proxies /api)
npm run dev:server            # http://127.0.0.1:8787
npm run worker                # separate terminal; does all media work
```

`server` scripts load `../.env` automatically when it exists.

Alternatively, set `NIX_OWNER_LOGIN`, `NIX_OWNER_DISPLAY_NAME` and `NIX_OWNER_PASSWORD` in the private `.env`. The API creates or synchronizes that administrator at startup. Change the password and recreate the app container with `docker compose up -d --force-recreate app`; a password change revokes that owner's existing sessions. Do not use this mode unless the `.env` is protected, because the password remains plaintext in the file and container environment.

For local use, point `NIX_MEDIA_ROOTS` at a folder you can read, for example:

```bash
NIX_MEDIA_ROOTS='[{"id":"photos","name":"Photos","path":"/path/to/photos"}]'
```

Then sign in as the admin and use **Admin → Media roots → Scan now**. Scans only read originals.

## Configuration

| Variable | Purpose | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `NIX_MEDIA_ROOTS` | JSON array of `{id,name,path}` roots. Administrative only; never client-supplied | empty |
| `NIX_OWNER_LOGIN`, `NIX_OWNER_DISPLAY_NAME`, `NIX_OWNER_PASSWORD` | Optional environment-managed administrator; all are disabled when the password is unset | unset |
| `NIX_DATA_DIR` | SQLite database, derivatives and temp workspace | `<repo>/data` |
| `NIX_DERIVATIVES_DIR`, `NIX_TEMP_DIR` | Override generated-asset locations | under data dir |
| `NIX_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` | Canonical origin for CSRF checks | derived from request |
| `NIX_COOKIE_SECURE` | `Secure` cookie flag | true in production |
| `NIX_TRUST_PROXY` | Honour `X-Forwarded-*` from the proxy; keep the app port loopback-only when enabled | false |
| `NIX_SESSION_IDLE_MINUTES` / `NIX_SESSION_ABSOLUTE_DAYS` | Session windows | 240 / 30 |
| `NIX_LOGIN_RATE_LIMIT_MAX` | Login attempts per minute per client | 10 |
| `NIX_STABILITY_WINDOW_SECONDS` | Skip files modified more recently (in-progress copies) | 60 |
| `NIX_SCAN_INTERVAL_MINUTES` | Scheduled reconciliation scan interval per root; `0` disables | `0` |
| `NIX_MIN_FREE_MB` | Free-space floor for derivative jobs; they retry rather than fill the volume | 512 |
| `NIX_FFMPEG_PATH`, `NIX_FFPROBE_PATH`, `NIX_EXIFTOOL_PATH` | Tool locations | `ffmpeg`, `ffprobe`, unset |
| `NIX_PUBLIC_DIR` | Built web bundle | `web/dist` |
| `NIX_HOST`, `NIX_PORT`, `NIX_LOG_LEVEL` | Listener and logging | `127.0.0.1`, `8787`, `info` |

## Docker

```bash
cp .env.example .env
docker compose build
docker compose run --rm -e NIX_ADMIN_LOGIN=owner -e NIX_ADMIN_PASSWORD='...' app node server/src/scripts/bootstrap-admin.ts
docker compose up -d
```

Compose runs the API and a separately limited worker from the same image. Media roots are mounted `:ro`; only `/data` is writable. Both services use a read-only root filesystem, drop all capabilities, set `no-new-privileges`, keep `/tmp` on a bounded tmpfs and run as `${PUID}:${PGID}`. Publish the app on loopback and terminate HTTPS in the existing DSM reverse proxy rather than exposing port 8787 publicly.

Production installation, DSM reverse proxy, certificate, backup scheduling, upgrade/rollback and the operator acceptance checklist are in the [Synology DS920+ runbook](docs/synology-deployment.md). Day-2 diagnosis is in [operations.md](docs/operations.md).

## HTTPS and reverse proxy

Terminate TLS in the existing DSM reverse proxy or an Nginx instance and keep the app on loopback; the app does not run its own public TLS listener. Set `NIX_PUBLIC_ORIGIN` to the exact external origin, `NIX_COOKIE_SECURE=true` and `NIX_TRUST_PROXY=true`. Forwarded headers are only safe while port 8787 stays unexposed, as the Compose file does. Configuration, DSM outline, verification checklist and limitations: [docs/https-proxy.md](docs/https-proxy.md).

## Backup

`npm run backup -- --out <file>` writes a consistent, verified SQLite snapshot while the API and worker keep running; `--force` replaces an existing file. The copy is checked for page integrity and foreign keys before it is published, and an interrupted run leaves nothing behind. Do not copy only `library.sqlite` while WAL is active. Restore steps are in [DATABASE.md](DATABASE.md#backup-and-restore).

## Verification

```bash
npm run lint
npm run typecheck
npm test
npm run build
```

`npm run lint` checks the server, worker, test suites, the Preact client and the service worker with ESLint's flat config and typescript-eslint; `npm run typecheck` compiles both workspaces without emitting. `npm test` covers authentication/authorization/CSRF/session revocation, path traversal and symlink escapes, job deduplication/leasing/retry/recovery, migrations and foreign keys, scan add/change/missing/unavailable-root behaviour, the probe + thumbnail pipeline, panorama tile generation/orientation/authorization, reverse-proxy trust and a local TLS terminator, HTTP Range/HEAD/416 semantics, PWA artifact/privacy boundaries, the disk reserve, and post-restore derivative regeneration. The current suite is 73 tests: 71 pass and 2 Windows symlink cases skip.

## Security notes

- No default credentials. Use the one-time bootstrap script, or explicitly configure the optional environment-managed owner. Environment-managed passwords must stay in the ignored, access-restricted `.env`.
- Sessions never live in `localStorage`; cookies are `HttpOnly` and `SameSite=Strict`.
- Every representation of a media item requires a session; ids resolve to validated server-side paths, and clients never send paths.
- Absolute host paths are not returned to clients.
- Private media is always `private, no-store`; no shared caching.

## Known limitations

- HDR tone-mapping is verified with a generated HLG/BT.2020 clip (the rendition is tagged BT.709), not with a real DJI D-Log M/HDR file or a device colour check. Panorama tiling, video resume, HLS, and remote-access guidance beyond the proxy note are not device-verified; video playback uses two precomputed MP4 profiles (no adaptive switching or HLS), and a quality switch has not been exercised in a browser.
- Rotated phone clips are catalogued and encoded upright from the container display matrix, but playing one in a browser or on a phone, and long-file encodes, remain unverified.
- Unreadable files and raw DJI OSV are catalogued with an explicit failed or conversion-required state, with guidance in the viewer; displaying those notices in a browser is not verified.
- The 360° viewer is limited to full 360 × 180 equirectangular photos at least 4096 px wide. Desktop Chrome tile loading, drag and reset were verified against one 15520 × 7760 DJI panorama; touch/pinch, gyroscope, fullscreen, repeated open/close, seam review at poles, and tile-generation timing on the NAS remain open.
- Raw DJI OSV (dual-fisheye HEVC) is preserved and flagged; it is not stitched or played.
- Without ExifTool, image capture dates fall back to file mtime (flagged uncertain) and GPS is absent.
- SQLite WAL requires the database to live on a local filesystem, not an SMB/NFS share.
- HTTPS is not deployed: only the application's proxy semantics and a local self-signed TLS terminator are verified. DSM/Nginx configuration, certificate issuance and renewal, external reachability and remote throughput remain untested.
- PWA installation, service-worker updates, offline shell behavior and offline-cache eviction are covered by build/artifact checks and local browser validation, not physical iOS/Android installation. HTTPS is required outside localhost.
- NAS, external bandwidth, and physical iOS/Android behaviour remain unverified.
